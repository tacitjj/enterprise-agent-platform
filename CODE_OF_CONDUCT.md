# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss technical decisions without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and personally identifiable information.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publication of private security details are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
